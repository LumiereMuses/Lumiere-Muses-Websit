Privacy Statement.

A clear explanation of the limited information we receive when you choose to contact us.

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Our approach.

Lumiere Muses Studio respects your privacy. This statement explains how we handle personal information when you use this website or contact us about our services.

Information you choose to share.

When you contact us through WhatsApp or email, we may receive information you choose to provide, including your name, phone number, email address, rental enquiry, requested equipment and preferred rental dates.

How we use it.

We use this information only to respond to enquiries, check equipment availability, arrange confirmed rentals and provide customer support. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising profiling.

Cookie notice.

This website uses one essential cookie, lumiere_muses_cookie_notice, after you accept the cookie notice. It remembers your preference for up to 12 months so the notice is not shown repeatedly.

We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies.

Third-party services.

Our website may link to WhatsApp, Instagram and Google. Those services operate under their own privacy policies once you leave our website or choose to contact us through their platforms.

Keeping information safe.

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer period is required by law. We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold.

Your choices.

You may ask to access, correct or withdraw consent for your personal data by contacting us at lumieremuses@icloud.com.

Updates.

We may update this Privacy Statement from time to time. The latest version will always appear on this website.
